  sink, 6.62 V with 1 % R; 8.60 V max); on = 1.24 × 6.556 + 0.3 = **8.43 V typ**, ≤ 9.15 V worst.  EN hysteresis ≥ 38 mV (≥ 0.25 V at
  the bus).  Below a sagged 4S pack (≥ 11 V at 3.3 V/cell and 22 A), and below the buck cut-off typ (9.2 V).  The EN sink current is
  3–5 µA and is included.  EN abs max (−0.3…65 V, or V(VS)…65 + V(VS) when reversed) is met.  The only error is the re-close corner
  (R6A-01).

  (R6A-03).

  stuck high in simulation (table in R6A-04).  Margins are in N-02.
