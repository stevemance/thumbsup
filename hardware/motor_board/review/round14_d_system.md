## 2. Checked and found sound (no finding)

* **Masked short through the supply class:** a real VDS OCP or phase short does not create a supply indicator.
  A 500 A × 4 µs short draws ≈ 2 mC, which the pack replaces in ~15–20 µs.  VBAT_SNS sees ≈ 0.05 V of it, and the
  INA239 VBUS barely moves.  So once R14D-01 is fixed, a short can pass as supply class only by coinciding with a
  genuine bounce, which is at most one extra event.  The comparator path is independent of the supply indicators.
* **Weapon auto-restart after supply events or a single comparator trip** stays inside the operator's ARM.  It
  never crosses a disarm: a W_ARM_S fall latches, and a new edge is required [D l.604].
* **DRV8316 single-chip events** (R13D-01 fix): bus evidence or both chips is required, and a lone chip is
  counted.  That is sound, apart from the SPI common mode (R14D-04) and the hold-off mechanics (R14D-03).
* **Two-step alignment and the motion check** (magnitude), the no-alignment-while-moving rule, the MT6701
  power-up train kept off, and the encoder speed cap: consistent.  The only gaps are the sign and the Z-trust
  issue (R14D-08).
* **Hot-plug and bounce:** hotplug.out re-read.  The worst case is 2.66 V/µs at C1's −40 °C limit, against 4 V/µs
  (§1 claims ≤ 2.75), 34 % margin.  Quick re-close is ≤ 2.09 V/µs.  Closure is ≤ 0.005 V/µs.  Bounces over
  ~1.3 ms open the switch (EN falls to 1.17 V after 1.32 ms with C18); after that the re-close is soft (C13 still
  charged, ≤ 0.06 V/µs).
* **Reversed pack:** D10 in place, 13 A C14 spike only.  **ARM:** arm.out gives 52–164 ms disarm and 7–10 edges
  to arm.
* **Compute-board reset or J1 unplugged:** R18 disarms; R16/R17 hold NRST and RX idle; the command timeout
  coasts.
* **Regen:** BOVL, the 18.5 V coast and the D1 clamp.  **§1 margins** are unchanged: 450 mA for the compute board;
  weapon VDS ≤ 29 V against 40 V.

**Verdict: 0 BLOCKER, 0 MAJOR, 8 MINOR, 3 NOTE.**  Every finding is firmware-contract, compute-board-contract or
bring-up text.  No hardware change is needed, and the hardware remains ready for schematic capture.  The

rewriting as an ordered table before firmware is written against it, or they will produce nuisance latch-offs
in a fight.
