## 3. Checked and found sound (no finding)

* **§1 margins, re-read against the outputs:**
  * Loaded bounce 2.26 V/µs (0 °C C1) and 2.66 V/µs (−40 °C) at the DRV8316 VM pins [S hotplug.out], against the §1 claim of ≤ 2.75 V/µs and the 4 V/µs limit.
  * Quick re-close ≤ 2.09 V/µs; closure ≤ 0.005 V/µs.
  * Disarm 52–164 ms; 7–10 edges to arm [S arm.out].
  * Spin-up: 22 A pack peak, 14.06 V minimum bus [S spinup.out].
  * Weapon VDS ≤ 29 V against 40 V.
  * D1 clamp 32.4 V against the DRV8316's 40 V.
  * 450 mA of 5 V for the compute board.
* **Per-drive coast via 6x** (INH = 0 gives a low side on in both modes; INH = INL = 1 is Hi-Z in 6x): no glitch, and ~15 µs of braking stays far below the 16 A OCP.  A latched chip that resets comes up in 6x with INH high, so it stays coasted.
* **A false bus-event slope** matters only when a break coincides with it.  Comparator breaks are classified by rows 1–2 before row 3, so a heavy load step cannot mask a comparator-caught short.  A phase-to-ground short costs at most one extra supply-classified event.
* **Operator-only clearing, the throttle-zero interlock and no automatic re-arm of a latch** (§3.5 vs §8.1) are now consistent.  Automatic restarts (rows 2, 3, 8, stall) stay inside a continuous operator ARM; a W_ARM_S fall always needs a new edge.
* **Weapon stall cycle** (0.5 s at 20 A, 1 s off) ≈ 10 W average in the motor, ~+50 K over a 3-minute match for a ~40 g 2822.  The bridge is bounded by the TH1 latch.

**Verdict: 0 BLOCKER, 0 MAJOR, 6 MINOR, 6 NOTE.**  §8.1 is now the right shape.  Every scenario ends safe: no reversed
drive and no operator-less re-arm.  The six MINORs are ordering and definition gaps in the decision procedure (plus
one unbounded compute-board rule) that cause a nuisance latch, a non-terminating branch, or a short reported as
trips.  All of them are firmware or compute-board contract text.  No hardware change is needed.
