## Verified correct (with evidence)

1. **Every U3/U4 pin matches the DRV8316R column of [DS] Table 6-1:** 1 NC, 2 AGND, 3 FB_BK, 4 GND_BK, 5 SW_BK, 6 CPL, 7 CPH, 8 CP, 9–11 VM, 12/15/18 PGND, 13–14 OUTA, 16–17 OUTB, 19–20 OUTC, 21 DRVOFF, 22 nFAULT, 23 nSLEEP, 24 NC, 25 AVDD, 26 AGND, 27/29/31 INHA/B/C, 28/30/32 INLA/B/C, 33 SDO, 34 SDI, 35 SCLK, 36 nSCS, 37 VREF/ILIM, 38 SOC, 39 SOB, 40 SOA, 41 pad → ground ("Must be connected to analog ground").
   - **R vs T differences:** pin 24 (R: NC, T: VSEL_BK) and pins 33–36 (R: SDO/SDI/SCLK/nSCS; T: MODE/SLEW/OCP/GAIN). The R variant is used (orderable DRV8316RRGFR, LCSC C5218861). The netlist uses the R functions on those pins.
2. **3x PWM with INLx tied high is TI-sanctioned.** "If the Hi-Z state is not required, tie all INLx pins to logic high" ([DS] §8.3.2.2, Table 8-4: INL = 1 → INH selects L/H). Braking = all INH low. Hi-Z/coast = DRVOFF high: "all six MOSFETs are disabled … irrespective of status of INHx and INLx" (§8.4.2).
3. **DRVOFF pull-up level:** R50 10 kΩ against 2 × internal 100 kΩ (70–130 kΩ) pull-downs gives 2.57–2.75 V, above V<sub>IH</sub> 1.5 V ([DS] 7.5). Both bridges are off while the MCU is in reset. PC14 sinks only 0.33 mA (limit 3 mA; [ST] Table 12 note 2).
4. **Power-up is safe:** +3V3 = 0 → nSLEEP low → "all FETs are disabled" (§8.4.1.1). When +3V3 is up, DRVOFF is high until firmware drives PC14 low. INHx have internal 100 kΩ pull-downs.
5. **Buck-unused connection is exactly TI's:** SW_BK → RBK 22 Ω → FB_BK, CBK 22 µF 6.3 V FB_BK → GND/GND_BK, pins not floating. [DS] §9.2.1.1.5: "Resistor Mode should be configured by placing a small value resistor of 22-ohm for RBK and a 6.3-V rated, 22-uF capacitor for CBK … set the BUCK_DIS"; Fig. 8-9; §8.3.4 note "cannot be left floating or connected to ground". FB_BK abs max is 5.75 V and VBK at the default BUCK_SEL = 00b is 3.1–3.5 V, so a 6.3 V cap is adequate.
6. **RBK dissipation before BUCK_DIS is written (22 Ω 0603, 0.1 W):**
   - **Steady state, no external load on VBK:** I<sub>BK</sub> is only the CBK leakage plus FB input current (µA). P ≈ (16.8 − 3.3) V × ~10 µA ≈ 0.1 mW.
   - **Start-up:** charging CBK to 3.3 V dissipates E = C·V<sub>BK</sub>·(VM − V<sub>BK</sub>/2) = 22 µF × 3.3 × 15.15 = **1.1 mJ** once, a temperature rise of about 1 K in a 0603.
   - **Peak pulse:** 16.8/22 = 0.76 A (12.8 W) for sub-µs PFM pulses, cut off by the 360–900 mA buck current limit. This is within the pulse capability of a thick-film 0603 (UNVERIFIED against the specific Uniroyal pulse curve).
   - **If AVDD were fed from VBK (D-17):** 13.5 V × 3–5 mA = 40–68 mW, which is 40–70 % of the rating. Hence the CTRL6 = 0x19 write.
   - **Reference points:** a 40 mA load would be 0.54 W, which is why the buck must stay unloaded. A shorted CBK would burn RBK open, a benign failure. TI's own sizing example is P > (VM − VBK)·I<sub>BK</sub> (Eq. 18–20).
7. **VREF/ILIM tied to AVDD is allowed:** ROC V<sub>VREF</sub> 2.8 V…AVDD ([DS] 7.3). AVDD can source 30 mA externally (Table 6-1), and the VREF load is ≤ 50 µA (I<sub>VREF</sub>). Mode 3 (3x, non-ILIM) uses the pin as VREF with SOx available (Table 8-2). An external 3.3 V LDO could exceed AVDD min 3.1 V, so the design's reasoning is right.
8. **CSA bias and gain:** SOx = VREF/2 ± GAIN·I (Eq. 21). CSA_GAIN reset = 00b = 0.15 V/A (Table 8-22), so no write is needed for 0.15 V/A. Offset ±50 mA, drift ±160 µA/°C.
9. **Charge pump:**
   - CP–VM 1 µF 50 V: TI asks for 16 V; V<sub>CP</sub> − VM ≤ 5.25 V.
   - CPH–CPL 47 nF 50 V: TI asks for ≥ 2 × VM = 33.6 V. ([DS] Table 6-1, Table 8-1)
   - VM 2 × 100 nF 50 V at pins 9 and 11 plus 10 µF 50 V: meets the "≥ 2× operating voltage" rule. The EVM uses a single 100 nF + 10 µF 63 V.
10. **VM ramp (4 V/µs abs max):** handled at board level by C1 470 µF polymer. spice/sim_hotplug.py reports 1.7–1.8 V/µs; that SPICE run is not reproduced here (outside this scope).
11. **nSLEEP tied high is legitimate.** Faults are cleared with CLR_FLT (§8.4.1.3). §8.3.10.1 recommends holding inputs low during sleep, and they are: INLx, DRVOFF and nSLEEP all come from +3V3, so they are low whenever the part sleeps.
12. **nFAULT pull-up 10 kΩ:** open drain, 1.8–5.5 V pull-up, I<sub>OD</sub> ≤ 5 mA (0.33 mA here). TI suggests 5.1 kΩ; 10 kΩ is fine. PB7 is TIM8_BKIN and FT_f; PC15 is FT.
13. **SPI bus sharing:** DRV8316, DRV8323 and INA229 all use CPOL = 0 / CPHA = 1 (mode 1).
    - DRV8316: "SCLK pin should be low when nSCS transitions … captured on the falling edge … propagated on the rising edge" ([DS] §8.5.1).
    - DRV8323: same wording ([D23] §8.5).
    - INA229: "MOSI sampled on the falling edge … MISO shifted out on the rising edge" ([INA] §7.5).
    - **No contention while deselected:** DRV8316 "SDO pin is placed in the Hi-Z state" when nSCS is high (push-pull by default, SDO_MODE = 1). INA229 "MISO output is tri-stated". DRV8323 SDO is open-drain.
    - DRV8316 nSCS has an internal 100 kΩ pull-up (7.5).
14. **Current capability:** 8 A peak rated (7.3). OCP_LVL default is 16 A (Table 8-21). R<sub>DS(on)</sub> is 95 mΩ typ at 25 °C, HS + LS (7.5). About 1 A running is comfortable at any slew setting (≤ 1 W).
15. **MT6701 at 3.3 V:** VDD 3.0–5.5 V, push-pull VOH ≥ 2.8 V at 3.3 V, above the STM32 V<sub>IH</sub>. PC6, PB5, PA15 and PB3 are FT/FT_f ([ST] Table 12). The ABZ → TIM CH1/CH2 encoder mode and UVW → Hall-interface (CH1–3 XOR) mapping is valid. The alternate functions were verified by the netlist script against ST's XML.
16. **J2/J3 pinout** (1 VS, 2 GND, 3–5 S1–S3, 6 TEMP, MP → GND) is self-consistent with the SM06B-SRSS-TB footprint in the KiCad library. Pre-made JST-SH cables are often pin-reversed, so check that when crimping.

## Summary of BLOCKER/MAJOR

- No BLOCKERs.
- **D-01 (MAJOR):** PB0 and PB10 (S3 inputs) are 3.6 V-tolerant TT_a pins with a 4.0 V abs max. The 5 V sensor-supply jumper position can destroy them. Fix: SN74LVC3G17 buffer per connector from +3V3, plus series R and pull-ups, or delete the 5 V option.
- **D-02 (MAJOR):** the sensor-connector VS comes directly off +3V3 or +5V. A shorted cable resets the whole robot. Fix: current-limited switch (TPS22945) or 22 Ω 0805 + 1 µF per connector, plus series R on the signal lines.
- **D-03 (MAJOR):** the drive thermal budget ignores slew-dependent switching loss and hot R<sub>DS(on)</sub>. At the reset-default 25 V/µs and the 4 A limit the loss is about 3.9 W, heading for thermal shutdown. Fix: SLEW = 200 V/µs with delay compensation (DLY_TARGET 0x5), and correct calcs.py and DESIGN.md.
