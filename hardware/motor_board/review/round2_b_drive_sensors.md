## VERIFIED OK

1. **U3/U4 pinout vs [C] Table 6-1 (DRV8316CR column), pin by pin:** 1 NC, 2 AGND, 3 FB_BK, 4 GND_BK, 5 SW_BK,
   6 CPL, 7 CPH, 8 CP, 9–11 VM, 12/15/18 PGND, 13–14 OUTA, 16–17 OUTB, 19–20 OUTC, 21 DRVOFF, 22 nFAULT,
   23 nSLEEP, 24 NC ("NC 1, 24 … No connection, open"), 25 AVDD, 26 AGND, 27/29/31 INHA/B/C, 28/30/32 INLA/B/C,
   33 SDO, 34 SDI, 35 SCLK, 36 nSCS, 37 VREF/ILIM, 38 SOC, 39 SOB, 40 SOA, pad "Must be connected to analog ground".
   A diff of the R and C pin tables shows only wording changes on the CT-only pins (MODE 4-level, OCP/SR). The package
   is the same RGF0040E, so the custom footprint carries over.
2. **Buck unused handling is required and correct for the C:** "the buck regulator components must be populated
   whether the buck is used or unused … 22-Ω for RBK … set the BUCK_DIS" ([C] §9.2.1.1.5, §8.3.4 note). Resistor
   mode requires BUCK_CL = 1b (Table 8-5), which is set by 0x19. Resistor mode is rated to 40 mA (IBK). R300/R400 are
   22 Ω 1206: before the write, the peak is (16.8 − 3.3)/22 = 0.61 A, below the 360–900 mA BUCK_CL = 0 limit, so the
   resistor sets the pulse current. After BUCK_DIS the dissipation is ~0. Only the CBK voltage rating changed (B-01).
3. **AVDD:** C305/C405 1 µF 50 V 0603 plus C306/C406 100 nF is about 0.8–1.0 µF effective at 3.3 V, inside TI's
   0.7–1.3 µF ([C] Table 8-1; "1-µF, ≥ 6.3-V"). External AVDD load is nFAULT pull-up 0.34 mA + I<sub>VREF</sub> ≤ 50 µA,
   far below 30 mA.
4. **CP and VM caps:** CCP 1 µF 50 V (TI 16 V), CFLY 47 nF 50 V (≥ 2 × VM = 33.6 V), and VM 2 × 100 nF + 2 × 10 µF
   50 V all meet [C] Table 8-1 / Figure 9-1. The VM ramp limit is still 4 V/µs abs max ([C] 7.1), handled by C1 (outside this scope).
5. **VREF/ILIM tied to its own AVDD:** VVREF ROC is 2.8 V…AVDD ([C] 7.3). In 3x mode (PWM_MODE 10b) the pin is the CSA
   reference and SOx is available (Table 8-2).
6. **nFAULT pull-up to its own AVDD (R301/R401 10 k):** open drain, 1.8–5.5 V pull-up, I<sub>OD</sub> ≤ 5 mA (0.35 mA
   here); TI suggests 5.1 kΩ. AVDD comes up with the chip, and "the nFAULT pin is held low as the internal regulators
   are enabled", so the > 2.2 V test-mode condition is met whenever the logic is alive. AVDD max 3.465 V is safe into
   PB7 (FT_f) and PC15 (FT). When +3V3 is absent, nSLEEP is low, so AVDD is off and nothing back-drives the MCU.
7. **INLx tied high in 3x mode is valid for the C:** "If the Hi-Z state is not required, tie all INLx pins to logic high"
   ([C] §8.3.2.2, Table 8-4, identical to the R). The 6x reset default with INL = 1 gives L/Hi-Z only (Table 8-3), and
   DRVOFF is high while firmware configures, as already covered by D-14.
8. **All register settings claimed in DESIGN §8 exist on the C** at the addresses and bits in the table above:
   CTRL6 = 0x19, BUCK_DIS, BUCK_PS_DIS, BUCK_CL, SLEW = 11b 200 V/µs, DLY_TARGET 0x5 (TI Table 8-6 value for
   200 V/µs), OVP_SEL (1 = 22 V), PWM_MODE reset 00b, CSA_GAIN 00b = 0.15 V/A (the reset default), SDO_MODE (reset 1 =
   push-pull, so no MISO pull-up is needed), and the even-parity bit B8.
9. **CSA filter R70–72/R80–82 330 Ω + C80–82/C90–92 22 pF C0G:** this is TI's recommendation in [C] §9.2.1.1.6. It is
   a series R into a capacitive load ("supports only capacitive load … TI recommends … RC"). L_SOC goes to OPAMP5
   VINP, which is high impedance. With the SOx range 0.25…AVDD − 0.25 V and saturation ≤ AVDD 3.465 V, the TT/FT pin
   ratings (≥ 4.0 V) hold.
10. **DRVOFF, nSLEEP and SPI electrical levels:** VIH 1.5 V (nSLEEP 1.6 V), inputs up to 5.5 V, internal pull-downs
    of 70–130 kΩ: unchanged from the R, so the round-1 analysis of R50 holds ([C] 7.5).
11. **U11/U12 TPS22945 pinout:** DCK 1 VOUT, 2 GND, 3 OC, 4 ON, 5 VIN, which matches the netlist and KiCad
    `SOT-353_SC-70-5` numbering. ON tied to VIN is legal: active high, "Do not leave floating", V<sub>IH</sub> 1.4 V at
    3.3 V and 1.7 V at 5.5 V, and V<sub>IN</sub> rising through UVLO gives a controlled turn-on (§9.1.2). OC is an
    open-drain output, so leaving it open is legal. 5 V operation: +5V is 4.93–5.16 V, within 5.5 V ROC and 6 V abs max. Short-circuit
    heating: 0.66 W (3.3 V) or 1 W (5 V) for 10 ms every 90 ms averages 73–110 mW, +21–33 °C at 294 °C/W. Two
    connectors in limit together add ≤ 400 mA to the ~120 mA +3V3 load, which is within the AP2112K's 600 mA.
12. **U9/U10 SN74LVC3G17DCUR pinout:** DCU 1 1A, 2 3Y, 3 2A, 4 GND, 5 2Y, 6 3A, 7 1Y, 8 VCC, which matches the netlist
    and KiCad `VSSOP-8_2.3x2mm_P0.5mm`. Inputs are rated to 5.5 V at any VCC (ROC V<sub>I</sub> 0–5.5 V, abs max 6.5 V).
    With the sensor powered and VCC unpowered, I<sub>off</sub> ≤ ±10 µA at V<sub>I</sub> = 5.5 V and VCC = 0 ("Ioff
    … preventing damaging current backflow"). In practice VS and VCC come from the same rails anyway.
13. **R54–R59 4.7 k to +3V3 with a 5 V push-pull sensor:** the back-current is (5.16 − 3.3)/4.7 k = 0.4 mA per line,
    2.4 mA with all six lines high. That is far below the ~120 mA +3V3 load, so the LDO simply sources less and no
    rail is lifted. A 5 V Hall board with its own 10 k pull-ups to VS gives ≈ 3.8 V at the input, which is fine. The
    MT6701 sinks 0.7 mA into the pull-up, within its 2 mA drive rating.
14. **MT6701 at 3.3 V and at 5 V:** VDD 3.0–5.5 V. At 3.3 V the budget is 3.25 V LDO min − 14 mA × 0.7 Ω, which leaves
    ≥ 3.2 V (> 3.0 V). VOH ≥ VDD − 0.5 = 2.8 V is above the Schmitt V<sub>T+</sub> max of 1.87 V (at VCC = 3 V). Idd is
    10/14 mA, far below the 100 mA limit. Edge rates are covered in N-09. Generic open-drain Halls (5 V jumper for ≥ 4.5 V types) pulled to 3.3 V
    swing 0–3.3 V, which is also above V<sub>T+</sub>.
15. **Motor NTC path:** 10 k pull-up + 10 k NTC. The sensitivity at 100 °C (B ≈ 3950) is about 5.6 mV/K, or 7 LSB/K.
    An open reads 3.3 V and a short reads 0 V, so both are detectable. PF0 (ADC1_IN10) and PF1 (ADC2_IN10) are slow
    channels with a 5 kΩ source and 100 nF, fine with 247.5-cycle sampling. Protection gaps are in B-05.
16. **XUNPU C3029345 is the right-angle SMD SH 1.0 6P** ("卧贴", horizontal SMD): 1 A, 50 V, −40…+85 °C, with the
    same 1.00 mm pitch and 5.00 mm pad span as SM06B-SRSS-TB. See B-06 for the land and pin-1 caveats.
