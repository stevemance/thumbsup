# Review r1: weapon channel (U2 DRV8323RS, Q1–Q6, buck) and power entry (Q7, D1, C1, U7)

Reviewer scope: the 48-pin DRV8323RS pinout and its support parts, the integrated LMR16006X buck, the weapon bridge
FETs, footprint, shunts and OCP, the reverse-polarity FET Q7, TVS D1, bulk C1, and the INA229 pack monitor.
Source of truth: `design/motor_board.py` (rev A, commit 302cf99). No design files were edited.

Datasheets used (all in `datasheets/`): DRV8323 = TI SLVSDJ3D (Mar 2022); LMR16006 = TI SNVSA24; INA229 = TI SLYS023A;
HYG015N04LS1C2 = HUAYI V1.0; SMNR4020 = SXN catalogue; B5819W = JCET; SMBJ20A = BORN; EEHZK = Panasonic ZK (Nov 2017);
BQ76907 = TI (only for the interaction with Q7). Page numbers are the datasheet's printed page numbers.

| W-01 | **BLOCKER** | U2.33 ENABLE, U6, W_ARM, W_EN | **Using ENABLE (sleep) as the weapon interlock does not work with an SPI DRV8323.** (a) In sleep the SPI bus is disabled, and every entry into sleep **resets all registers to defaults**. Because ENABLE = W_EN AND W_ARM, U2 is asleep until the compute board arms, so the boot order in DESIGN §8 ("configure drivers over SPI → … enable W_EN only after W_ARM") cannot be followed for U2. It has to be reconfigured, at least 1 ms after it wakes, every time either signal goes low. (b) W_ARM goes only to U6.B, J1.7 and R41 (nets.md). **The MCU never sees W_ARM**, so it cannot tell when U2 has been reset. If the compute board drops and then re-asserts W_ARM (link glitch, disarm/re-arm, connector chatter) while TIM1 is running, U2 wakes in the defaults **6x PWM, IDRIVEP/N = 1000/2000 mA**. The MCU's 3x-style inputs (INL = 1, INH = PWM) then decode in 6x mode as "INL=1, INH=0 → low-side ON" and "INL=1, INH=1 → Hi-Z". That applies low-side braking pulses on all phases at the gate current that `spice/sim_weapon_bridge.py` shows overshooting the 40 V FETs (400 mA already gives 36–44 V). Only nFAULT (held low during wake) and the firmware's TIM1 AOE setting stand in between. (c) **Disarm while spinning** (the compute board's failsafe case) drops ENABLE asynchronously to the PWM. TI requires INHx/INLx to be low within 40 µs of ENABLE falling. Otherwise the gates follow the inputs for 50 µs, then **go Hi-Z while possibly high** until nFAULT goes low (up to 400 µs). During that time a gate that was on stays charged through only the 150 k / 480 k hold-off resistors, with OCP no longer active and the drum at up to 25 k rpm. The MCU gets only the delayed nFAULT edge, so it cannot meet the 40 µs rule. DESIGN §3.2 ("ENABLE low … all six gates held low") is therefore wrong. (d) An 8–40 µs low glitch on W_ARM is a valid "ENABLE reset pulse" and **silently clears latched faults**, including a VDS OCP latch. | SLVSDJ3D §8.4.1.1 p.51: "In sleep mode … the charge pump is disabled, the DVDD regulator is disabled, and the SPI bus is disabled"; Note: "The INHx and INLx pins should be low before tRST (max 40μs) after ENABLE goes low to prevent the GHx and GLx outputs from entering into Hi-Z state while any of the gates are high"; "GHx and GLx … will become Hi-Z until nFAULT goes low up to 400μs after ENABLE is low". §8.5.1.1 p.53: "The SPI registers are reset to the default settings on power up, when the device enters sleep mode, and when a UVLO fault occurs." ## Verified correct (with evidence)

**U2 DRV8323RS pin map.** All 49 connections in `motor_board.py` match SLVSDJ3D Table 6-4 (48-pin DRV8323R, "DRV8323RS" column, pp.10–11):
1 FB, 2 PGND, 3 CPL, 4 CPH, 5 VCP, 6 VM, 7 VDRAIN, 8 GHA, 9 SHA, 10 GLA, 11 SPA, 12 SNA, 13 SNB, 14 SPB, 15 GLB, 16 SHB, 17 GHB,
18 GHC, 19 SHC, 20 GLC, 21 SPC, 22 SNC, 23 SOC, 24 SOB, 25 SOA, 26 VREF, 27 DGND, 28 nFAULT, 29 SDO, 30 SDI, 31 SCLK, 32 nSCS,
33 ENABLE, 34 CAL, 35 AGND, 36 DVDD, 37 INHA, 38 INLA, 39 INHB, 40 INLB, 41 INHC, 42 INLC, 43 BGND, 44 CB, 45 SW, 46 NC,
47 VIN, 48 nSHDN, thermal pad → GND. Pins 29–32 are the SPI functions (in the RH variant they are MODE/IDRIVE/VDS/GAIN), so the S variant is required, as BOM.md says.

**U2 support parts.**
- C20 47 nF 50 V X7R across CPH–CPL: TI asks for "47-nF, VM-rated … 50V (2x margin)" in a 24 V system. It sees ≤ VM, so 50 V covers 16.8 V and a 32 V clamp.
- C22 1 µF on DVDD: TI asks for 1 µF 6.3 V.
- C23 100 nF on VREF.
- C24 100 nF plus C25/C26 10 µF on VM: TI asks for 0.1 µF plus ≥ 10 µF.
- C27 2.2 µF 50 V on VIN: identical to TI's SNVSA24 §9.2.2.5 design.
- C28 100 nF 50 V CB–SW: TI asks for 0.1 µF ≥ 16 V.
- VDRAIN (7) on VBAT, Kelvin to the drains (DESIGN §6.2).
- NC (46) left floating is allowed.
- CAL = GND: SPI devices calibrate with CSA_CAL_x.

**VREF = +3V3.** This is within the 3–5.5 V recommended range (§7.3), and IVREF of 2–3 mA fits the AP2112K budget. Bidirectional mode (VREF_DIV = 1, the default) biases SOx at 1.65 V. The linear range is 0.25 to VREF − 0.25 V, so with gain 20 and 2 mΩ (40 mV/A) the range is ±35 A, which matches calcs.md. SOx ≤ VREF + 0.3 V, which is safe for the ADC. Readings are ratiometric with VDDA/VREF+ through R60.

**CSA input ranges.**
- VDIFF ±0.3 V = ±150 A.
- VCOM ±0.15 V: SPx = +40 mV at 20 A, so the CSA reads correctly up to ~75 A.
- SPx/SNx abs max ±1 V continuous = ±500 A, and ±3 V for 200 ns.
- Pack/lead-limited short-circuit current (~240 A → 0.48 V) stays inside the abs max.
- SNx to the shunt ground pad through a net-tie, and SPx to the FET-source side (W_SLx), is exactly the wiring in Table 6-4 ("SPx … Connect to the low-side power MOSFET source and high-side of the current shunt resistor").

**Gate drive and charge pump.**
- The IDRIVE codes exist: 60 mA source = 0010b, 120 mA sink = 0010b (Tables 8-16/8-17).
- Charge-pump budget: 3 × Qg(59 nC) × 24 kHz = 4.2 mA against IVCP 25 mA at VM 13 V (20 mA at VM 10 V) and IGATE_HS ≤ 25 mA average. OK.
- At a 10 V pack, VGSH ≥ 6.3 V, and RDS(on) at 4.5 V is 2.4 mΩ max, so the FETs are still enhanced.
- Dead time: 3x mode inserts it with the VGS handshake (§8.3.1.4.2). No MCU dead time is needed.
- 3x truth table (Table 8-3): INLx = 0 → both gates low, which supports timer-controlled Hi-Z.

**nFAULT/SDO pull-up voltage.** 3.3 V is within VOD ≤ 5.5 V. SPI mode 1 is common to DRV8323, DRV8316 and INA229.

**Integrated buck.**
- FB: 0.765 × (1 + 56k/10k) = 5.05 V. With VFB 0.747–0.782 V and 1 % resistors the output is 4.87–5.22 V, which is fine for the AP2112K and USB-class 5 V loads.
- fSW is 595–805 kHz (the LMR16006X), so L = 22 µH and Lmin = 20.9 µH at 16.8 V (calcs.md) are consistent. Ripple is 0.23 A p-p, giving 0.71 A peak at a 0.6 A load.
- The buck runs independently of ENABLE: §8.4.1.1 says "The LMR16006X buck regulator … is not controlled by the ENABLE pin".

**nSHDN UVLO, recomputed.** Model: 1 µA pull-up while off, 4.2 µA while on (SNVSA24 §7.5).
- Typical threshold 1.25 V: **on 10.42 V / off 9.17 V**.
- Threshold 1.05 V: on 8.69 V / off 7.44 V.
- Threshold 1.38 V: on 11.54 V / off 10.29 V.

This matches DESIGN and calcs.md. The worst-case off level of 10.3 V is below the 13.7 V spin-up bus minimum. The pin voltage at 16.8 V is 2.13 V, well under its VIN limit.

**D2 in normal operation.** 40 V is ≥ 1.25 × 16.8 V (SNVSA24 §9.2.2.4). The only problem is the fault case (W-03).

**5 V budget.** 114 mA for the 3.3 V side, plus ~3 mA for the LED, plus 456 mA for the compute board, is about 0.57 A of the 0.6 A rating. Buck and driver dissipation inside U2 is < 0.5 W, about +13 °C at 26.6 °C/W. The budget is tight but consistent with calcs.md §4.

**HYG015N04LS1C2 on PQFN-8-EP_6x5mm_P1.27mm_Generic.**
- Datasheet p.1 pin description (top view, pin 1 marked): S S S G on the lead side and D D D D opposite. Package drawing p.7: PDFN5×6, e = 1.27, D2 4.25 / E2 3.82, with the drain leads fused to the pad.
- KiCad footprint: pads 1–4 in one column at x = −2.725, y = −1.905 … +1.905 (pitch 1.27). Pad 5 is a custom polygon covering the EP and the opposite (drain) lead row, x −1.1 … +3.5, width 4.7 mm.
- So leads 1–3 = S, 4 = G, 5 = tab/D is correct for Q1–Q7.
- Ratings: VGS ±20 V; 40 V / IDM 600 A; body diode IS 150 A; Crss 33 pF; Ciss 4050 pF.

**Q7 reverse-polarity FET.** Drain = BAT−, source = GND, so the body diode's anode is at GND and it is forward-biased for normal pack current: correct low-side RPP orientation. Scenarios checked:
- **Plug-in.** The body diode carries the C1 surge. That is ≈ 0.9 V × 510 µF × 16.8 V ≈ 8 mJ in the diode, trivial against IS 150 A. The gate charges through 100 k × ~4.1 nF (τ ≈ 0.42 ms), crossing Vth(max) 2.5 V after ≈ 70 µs and reaching ~10 V after ≈ 0.4 ms. Nothing heavy is loaded in that time (MCU boot and driver config take ms).
- **Steady state.** Vgs = min(VBAT, ~11–12 V zener). At 48 µA the MMSZ5242B sits below its 12 V IZT rating (VZ 11.4–12.6 V @ 20 mA), so Vgs ≈ 10–12 V, within the ±20 V rating. At the 9.2 V cutoff Vgs is still 9 V, so RDS(on) ≤ 1.7 mΩ.
- **TVS event.** At 32 V the zener current is (32 − 12)/100 k = 0.2 mA and the gate is clamped at ~12 V.
- **Reversed pack.** BAT− is at +16.8 V and board GND floats near VBAT (at most one forward diode, D1, above it). R1 pulls the gate toward VBAT ≤ GND, D4 clamps Vgs ≥ −0.7 V, so Q7 is off and blocks 16.8 V against its 40 V rating. D1 cannot conduct because its only return path is through the off Q7 (DESIGN claim confirmed).
- **Miller kick at reversed plug-in.** Crss/Ciss ≈ 33/4050, so ΔVg ≈ 0.14 V, far below Vth(min) 1.2 V.
- **Balance lead with a reversed pack.** This is a path and is already documented in DESIGN §7.1. The forward-pack interaction is W-10.

**Q7 and U7.** U7 is referenced to board GND on the high side, so Q7's drop does not enter the shunt measurement: VCM = VBAT is within −0.3 to 85 V.

**D1 SMBJ20A.**
- VRWM 20 V > 16.8 V.
- VC 32.4 V at 18.6 A is below the DRV8316's 40 V abs max, the DRV8323's VM/VDRAIN/VIN 65 V abs max, and the INA229's 85 V common-mode / VBUS abs max.
- Placement after Q7 is correct.
- In practice it rarely conducts. The inductive kick from a 22 A interruption in ~1 µH of lead (≈ 0.24 mJ) is absorbed by C1 with negligible ΔV.

**C1.** 470 µF / 20 mΩ / 2.8 A rms at 100 kHz and 125 °C, in the G case (10 × 10.2). The KiCad CP_Elec_10x10.5 footprint is dimensionally compatible, but confirm it against the ZK land pattern. For the ~8 A rms weapon ripple bursts (calcs.md §7), the 0.5 s duration is short against the thermal time constant, so this is acceptable for bursts. The voltage-rating issue is W-06.

**U7 INA229 pinout.** 1 CS, 2 MOSI, 3 ALERT, 4 MISO, 5 SCLK, 6 VS, 7 GND, 8 VBUS, 9 IN−, 10 IN+ (SLYS023A Table 5-1) match the netlist. Other checks:
- IN+ is on the supply side of RS4 (VBAT_PACK) and IN− on the load side (VBAT), which is correct for high-side sensing.
- VS = 3.3 V is within 2.7–5.5 V. ALERT and MISO are within VS + 0.3 V.
- MISO is push-pull but tri-stated when CS is high (§7.5.1), so it is safe on the shared bus.
- Input filter: 10 Ω + 10 Ω with 100 nF differential gives fc ≈ 80 kHz. This matches §8.1.4 ("lowest possible series resistance (typically 100 Ω or less) … 0.1 µF and 1 µF" and "10-Ω resistors in series with each input … protects the inputs against this dV/dt failure"). The gain error from RDIFF 92 kΩ is ≈ 0.02 %.
- Range: ADCRANGE = 1 is ±40.96 mV, i.e. ±41 A at 1 mΩ with 78 µA/LSB. That covers the 22–27 A pack peak, and an SOVL of 35 A still trips when the reading saturates.
- RS4 dissipates 0.48 W at 22 A against its 3 W rating.

**U6 74LVC1G08 (SOT-353).** A = 1, B = 2, Y = 4 per the Diodes logic diagram, with GND = 3 and VCC = 5 (standard SC-70-5). The output VOH of 3.3 V exceeds DRV8323 VIH 1.5 V. This is only relevant if U6 is kept (see W-01).

**L1 land pattern.** The SMNR4020 recommended land (pads 3.3 × 0.95 mm, gap 2.1 mm, so pad centres at ±1.53 mm) is compatible with KiCad L_Changjiang_FNR4020S (1.1 × 3.7 mm pads at ±1.5 mm).
