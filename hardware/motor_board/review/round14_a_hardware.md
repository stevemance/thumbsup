## Independent re-checks (no finding)

* **Regeneration and sims**: identical outputs (above).  Bounce rows: 0.8–2.26 V/µs at VM, 2.66 V/µs at the −40 °C C1
  limit; re-close worst 2.09 V/µs; ARM disarm 52–164 ms.  All match §1/§3/§5/§7.2.
* **Fault-action facts** used in §8:
  * U2 UVLO → gates Hi-Z, nFAULT low, automatic recovery ([DRV23] Table 8-7 p.48).
  * VDS OCP on the H device → 4 ms auto-retry, reduced-current turn-off ([DRV23] §8.3.6.3 p.49).
  * GDF is the only latched fault, cleared by an ENABLE pulse of 8–40 µs (t_RST, p.16).
  * CPUV is always enabled on H devices (§8.3.6.2).
  * DRV8316 OCP_LVL 0 = 10–22 A (p.13).
  * DRVOFF disables all six FETs independently of INx ([DRV16] §8.4.2 p.53).
* **SOVL arithmetic** (R14A-03): the loaded-bounce recharge is 374 µF × ~11 V ≈ 4.1 mC.  Averaged over one 150 µs
  conversion that is ~27 A on top of any remaining load, so SOVL (38 A) is likely but not certain for 0.3–0.8 ms bounces.
  A 0.1 ms bounce (bus to ~9–10 V, no U2 UVLO) gives ~15 A + load, and is borderline.

**Verdict: 0 BLOCKER, 1 MAJOR, 3 MINOR, 2 NOTE.**  The netlist, the sims and the R302/TAMP/boot-test physics are right.
No hardware change is required, and all four findings are contract text.
* The per-drive "coast" via TIM8/TIM20 MOE is physically a brake, because INL is hard-tied high.  Rewrite it as a
  zero-current stop with OIS = 1, and make the DRV_OFF actions consistent.
* The VBAT-dip rule misclassifies overload SOVLs as supply events and misses 0.2–0.3 ms bounces.
* The W_nFAULT timing needs repeated DIAG_ALRT reads, and a measured tRETRY.
* The lost-encoder probe false-trips a stalled pushing wheel.
