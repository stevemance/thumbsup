## Verified (no finding)

**SPI words** (SLVSH07 §8.5.1.1: B15 W = 0 for a write, B14–B9 address, B8 set so the word has even parity, B7–B0 data; register fields from Tables 8-18, 8-19 and 8-21, pp.62–65).

| Word | Reg | Data decode | Ones |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0x0603 / 0x0606 | CTRL1 (3h) | REG_LOCK 011b unlock / 110b lock | 4 / 4 |
| 0x0D10 | CTRL4 (6h) | DRV_OFF 0, OCP_CBC 0, OCP_DEG 01b = 0.6 µs, RETRY 0, OCP_LVL 0 = 16 A, OCP_MODE 00b latched | 4 |
| 0x097D | CTRL2 (4h) | bits 7–6 = 01b (reserved, reset 1h), SDO_MODE 1 push-pull, SLEW 11b = 200 V/µs, PWM_MODE 10b = 3x, CLR_FLT 1 (W1C, self-clearing) | 8 |
| 0x0C90 / 0x0D94 / 0x0C14 | CTRL4 | coasted 16 A / coasted 24 A / released 24 A | 4 / 6 / 4 |
| 0x087C, 0x0A4E, 0x1019, 0x0F00, 0x1818 | CTRL2/3/6/5/10 | unchanged since round 26 | 6 / 6 / 4 / 4 / 4 |

The Release text is identical in all three places: §8.1 "drives resume" step 2 (l.675), step 3 (l.676), and the §8 DRV8316 row (l.612, base form without CLR_FLT).  In each, CLR_FLT follows 0x0D10 inside the unlock/lock bracket.  The 24 A variant substitutes 0x0C14 correctly.

The step 4 recover (0x0603 → 0x0C90 → 0x097D → 0x0606) clears with the DRV_OFF bit set.  That is fine: it then repeats from step 2, whose Release carries the post-0x0D10 CLR_FLT.  The CTRL2 rewrite keeps PWM_MODE = 3x, so the mode does not change while the FETs operate (SLVSH07 §8.3.2 note).

**Open-bus decay ~0.27 V/ms.**  The idle draw is 1.6–1.9 W (calcs §9).  At 18–19 V that is ~85–105 mA, into 374 µF: 0.23–0.28 V/ms.  With C1 at its −20 % tolerance it is up to ~0.33 V/ms.

Row 1 reads as "looks open" when VBUS stays above ~18 V:

* After a BOVL alert (≥ 19 V) the margin is ~0.7 V.
* After the 18.5 V firmware coast, the second post-coast conversion lands ≤ ~0.9 ms later.  The bus is then ≥ 18.5 − 0.33 × 0.9 ≈ 18.2 V, still > 18 V.

The INA239 VBUS node is VBAT after RS4 (netlist `VBAT,U7,8`), so the reading is the bus, not BAT_IN.

**D1 clamp and sense-pin rating (l.609).**

* D1 is the SMBJ20A (BORN datasheet electrical table): VBR 22.2–24.5 V at 1 mA, and VC 32.4 V at IPP 18.6 A.
* The linear dynamic resistance is (32.4 − 24.5)/18.6 = 0.425 Ω.  At 10 A that gives 24.5 + 4.25 = **28.7 V** at 25 °C.
* The dividers are R63/R64 and R22–R27, 68 k/10 k at 1 % (bom.csv l.40–41), so ÷7.8.  At 28.7 V that is 3.69 V nominal and 3.75 V with worst-case tolerances.
* PA3, PA4 and PA5 (LQFP-64 pins 17/18/19) are TT_a, and PA2 is FT_a (DS12288 pin table).  The TT abs max is 4.0 V (Table 14).
* Positive injection "is not possible on these I/Os and does not occur for input voltages lower than the specified maximum" (Table 15 note 3), so there is no cross-channel effect below 4.0 V.
* The phase dividers' 1 nF (τ ≈ 8.7 µs) does not filter a ~0.1–0.8 ms clamp.  During the clamp the pins follow ≤ bus/7.8, as round 26 said.

The statement is correct as a nominal figure.  Its margin is the subject of R27A-01.

**Other nodes at the 28.7 V clamp** (the text already covers ≤ 32.4 V; checked for completeness):

* U13 LM74502: VS and EN/UVLO abs max 65 V (LM74502 abs max table, "VS to GND", "EN/UVLO, OV to GND").  EN/UVLO sees 3.7–4.2 V via R13/R14.
* U2 nSHDN: abs max −0.3 V to V_VIN (DRV8323 abs max table).  It sees 3.3 V via R4/R5.
* The INA239 bus/shunt inputs, rated 85 V.
* The DRV8316 VM: 40 V abs max, and its OVP Hi-Zs the drives at 20–22 V.
* The capacitors: C1 35 V, C14 100 V, the VM caps 50 V.
* VBAT_SNS_H reaches the compute board at ≤ 3.7 V through R33 100 k.

**Regen current and TVS energy.**  The regen current is 10 A less ~0.1 A of idle, into ~374 µF: ~26 V/ms.  D1 conducts ≤ 10 A for ≤ ~0.5 ms after BOVL.  A bus conversion that straddles the 19 V crossing can average below 19 V, so the alert comes ≤ ~0.35–0.45 ms after the crossing.  That is ≤ ~0.15 J, inside the 600 W 10/1000 µs rating.

**Pause / coast / §9 wording.**  The round-26 row 0 wording ("regenerating torque command") matches the hardware facts it relies on:

* RS4 carries all VBAT load and none of the BAT_IN-side load.
* With the switch open, |I| ≈ 0.2 mA.

## Findings

### R27A-01 (MINOR) — the TT-pin margin now rests on the *actual* pack-side regen current being ≤ ~10 A, and nothing in §9 measures it

l.609 now reads: "combined regen limited by a **current** limit (≤ ~10 A: with the switch open during a brake the TVS then clamps ≤ ~28.7 V, keeping the 4.0 V-rated sense pins PA3–PA5 at ≤ ~3.7 V)".  That limit is a firmware estimate of DC-side current, taken from the FOC voltages and currents.  The pin voltage depends on the real current into D1.

The pin voltage (and the phase-sense pins, which follow the bus) versus the regen current into D1:

| Regen into D1 | D1 at 25 °C | Pin, nominal ÷7.8 | Pin, 1 % worst |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 A | 28.7 V | 3.69 V | 3.75 V |
| 12 A | 29.6 V | 3.79 V | 3.86 V |
| 15 A | 30.9 V | 3.96 V | **4.03 V** |
| 18.6 A | 32.4 V | **4.15 V** | **4.23 V** |

- The pins reach the 4.0 V abs max at **~14.5 A** of regen with worst-case dividers, or ~15.8 A nominal.  That is only ~45–60 % above the design value.
- VBR rises with temperature.  The BORN sheet gives no αVBR, but the typical SMBJ figure is ~0.1 %/°C, so +35 °C adds ~0.9 V.  That brings the threshold down to ~12–13 A.
- A 20–30 % error in the estimate, which is plausible for a sensorless weapon observer at 25 k rpm and for the drives' Vq·Iq products, therefore uses up most of the margin.
- The only §9 check on the brake is "log INA239 VBUS: the peak must stay < 18.3 V" (l.762).  With R_pack + switch ≈ 74 mΩ that bounds the regen current only to (18.3 − 16.8)/0.074 ≈ **20 A**, not 10 A.  A mis-scaled regen limit would pass it and then put ~4.1–4.2 V on PA3–PA5 the first time the switch opens mid-brake.
- It is a rare event (switch open during a hard brake), and it lasts ≤ 0.5 ms.  But it is an abs-max violation on the ADC pins that carry the bus and weapon-phase sensing, and the text now presents 10 A as the thing that prevents it.

Fix (text only, §9 step 6).  In the existing "brake the drum at the chosen regen limit" test, also log the INA239 current.  With the switch closed, RS4 carries exactly the current D1 would take with it open (regen minus the ~0.1 A idle).  Require the most negative reading to be ≥ ~−10 A, weapon and drives combined (repeat with the drives braking too), else lower the limit.  Optionally, state in l.609 that the ~10 A is the *measured* pack-side value.

### N1 (NOTE) — weapon FET VDS during the D1 clamp window at the 6 nH layout bound

The bridge keeps switching regen until the BOVL break, for up to ~0.1–0.2 ms at a clamped 25–28.7 V bus.  `sim_weapon_bridge.py` rerun at VBAT = 28.7 V with 60 mA IDRIVE:

| Loop inductance | High-side VDS | Low-side VDS | SH min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 nH | 36.0–36.3 V | 32 V | −2.8 V |
| 6 nH | **42.3–42.9 V** | 34.5 V | −5.1 V |

The same run at 16.8 V gives 23.7 V (3 nH) and 29.4 V (6 nH), which reproduces DESIGN §5.  At the ≤ 6 nH rule, the high-side FET therefore rings ~3 V past its 40 V VDSS for a handful of edges.

The HYG015N04LS1C2 is "100 % avalanche tested", with EAS = 370 mJ (datasheet features and abs max table).  The ring energy is ≲ ½ × 6 nH × (20 A)² ≈ 1.2 µJ per edge, so this is harmless, and SH stays within −7 V.  The 3 nH target gives 36 V (no avalanche).

This is one more reason to hold the 3 nH target.  No change is needed.  The model is not a vendor model, so read the numbers as trends (§5).

### N2 (NOTE) — the §9 step 4 idle pass line sits inside the design's own idle range

The line is "pass ≥ ~100 mA" (l.748).  calcs §9 gives 1.6–1.9 W idle, which is **95–113 mA at 16.8 V**, where the constant-power buck draws the least.  A healthy board with a light compute board could read ~85–95 mA and "fail".

Row 0 needs only two things:

* The real idle must sit clearly above the 30 mA hold threshold.
* The stored idle term must exceed the 50 mA estimate band.

The estimate is firmware's own stored constant, so ~70–80 mA already works.  Consider "pass ≥ ~2× the band (≥ ~100 mA), or narrow the band to ~idle/2 otherwise", so that a fail has a stated action.  Round 26 suggested exactly this, and the fail action was dropped.

### N3 (NOTE) — §3.2 "the 1 nF also keeps a TVS-level spike below the 4 V abs max of PA4/PA5" (l.184)

This holds only for spikes much shorter than τ ≈ 8.7 µs.  A D1 clamp (0.1–0.8 ms) passes straight through the filter.  At D1's full 32.4 V rating the pins would see 4.15 V, which is what R27A-01 is about.

l.609 now carries the real constraint (the regen current limit).  A cross-reference ("sustained D1 clamps: see the §8 regen limit") would stop a reader from taking the 1 nF as the protection.  Round 26 N2 suggested placing this note here, and it went to §8 instead.
