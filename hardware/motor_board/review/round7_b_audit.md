## Verdict

BLOCKER 0, MAJOR 1, MINOR 7, NOTE 6.  Connections, pinouts, diode polarities (including D10), footprints, BOM totals and classes are
clean.  The open items are the re-close model's gate-source dead band, which rev G's R32 turned into an early turn-off (R7B-01), and
stale or contradictory rev G figures in the netlist comments, calcs, DESIGN §3.5/§4 and the spice docs (R7B-02…08).
