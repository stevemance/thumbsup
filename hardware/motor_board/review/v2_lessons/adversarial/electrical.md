# Adversarial review: electrical and stack-up rules for the v2 4-layer restart

Scope: README.md §3 (the merged rules), electrical.md (B, C, D, E) and critique.md §8. The sources checked were
DESIGN.md §2-§6, design/calcs.md, FAB.md and PLACEMENT.md §3/§5. The JLC stack-ups below come from JLC's own
templates as exported by `gsuberland/jlcpcb_autogenerated_stackups`. JLC's capability page and its POFV announcement
were also checked (links at the end).

## 0. The stack-up JLC actually builds (this drives most findings)

| JLC 4L, 1 oz outer / 1 oz inner | L1-L2 prepreg | L2-L3 core | L3-L4 prepreg | inner Cu (finished) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| JLC041611-1080 (1.6 mm) | 0.069 | **1.23** | 0.069 | 0.030 mm |
| JLC041611-3313 (1.6 mm) | 0.092 | **1.23** | 0.092 | 0.030 |
| JLC041611-7628 (1.6 mm) | 0.203 | 1.03 | 0.203 | 0.030 |
| JLC041211-1080 / -3313 (1.2 mm) | 0.069 / 0.092 | **0.83** | 0.069 / 0.092 | 0.030 |
| JLC041211-2116 (1.2 mm) | **0.193** (2 plies) | 0.70 | 0.193 | 0.030 |
| JLC041612-3313 (2 oz inner) | **0.2005** (2 plies) | 0.96 | 0.2005 | 0.061 |
| JLC041621-3313 (2 oz outer) | 0.092 | 1.23 | 0.092 | 0.030 |

The memo is right that thin prepreg exists on 4 layers. Two facts are missing:

- **Every thin 4L build is symmetric, with a 0.8-1.2 mm core between L2 and L3.**
- **1 oz inner copper is 30 µm finished**, and 0.5 oz is 15.2 µm.

The memo's reasoning (L3 VBAT paired with L2, L3 GND as a plane that is interchangeable with L2) carries over from
the 6-layer JLC061611-1080B. In that build L2-L3 was 0.075 mm. Here L3 is electromagnetically paired with **L4**,
not L2.

## Findings

Severity is in each heading.

### 1. L3 VBAT pairs with L4, not L2 (critical)

**Evidence.** §0: L3-L4 is 0.069-0.092 mm and L2-L3 is 0.83-1.23 mm. Electrical.md B justifies L3 VBAT by
"L3 as reference for L4" and FAB.md's "L3 VBAT pour against L2 adds plane capacitance". That was a 6L property.

**Consequences.**

- **AC return of the C1-to-cell feed.** Ripple current on the L3 VBAT feed (C1 plus three cell MLCCs sharing
  ~8 A rms, chopped at 24 kHz, calcs §7) returns on whatever GND lies closest.
  - Over the L2 return, the loop is ~1.2 mm tall: μ0·h·l/w ≈ 6 nH for 40 x 10 mm.
  - Over an L4 GND fill, it is 0.07 mm tall: ≈ 0.35 nH.
  - So if L4 is filled, the bottom layer carries pack ripple return. That undermines DESIGN §6.10 (no pack current
    on the bottom face) and the PLACEMENT §8 plane-drop solve, which models L2 only.
- **Reference for L4 signals.** If L4 is not filled there, every L4 signal in the band references VBAT. This hits
  the gate/Kelvin corridor (electrical rule 5 "conflict to resolve") and the SPx/SNx/VDRAIN runs to U2. Each return
  must jump VBAT to GND through a capacitor at the band edge.
- **L2-L3 via stubs cut the loop flux.** Every signal via crossing the band from L1 to L4 passes through the 1.2 mm
  core, where the L3-feed/L2-return loop's flux lives. This matters most for the Kelvin and gate vias.

**Correction.** Pick this explicitly in the rules:

- (a) **Front band = L3 VBAT over a solid L4 GND pour with no signals.** Stitch it to L2 at C1's GND, at each shunt
  GND pad and every ~3 mm along the band edge. Record in DESIGN §6.10 that L4 carries AC return current under mask
  (a shield, not exposed pack copper). Then all gate/Kelvin/VDRAIN/SPx/SNx routing crosses the band on **L1 only**,
  or on L4 only over L3 GND.
- (b) Shrink the L3 VBAT band to a feed strip (e.g. y 1.5-9). The U2 corridor then runs on L4 over L3 GND.

Also require that **no L4 trace crosses an L3 VBAT/GND boundary**. Where one must, put a 100 nF VBAT-GND
capacitor within 2 mm of the crossing. Add this as a DRC or script check.

### 2. VBAT via fields and U1 fan-out rows slot L2 (critical)

**Evidence.** Critique §8 puts VBAT/GND via fields at "0.3 mm drill, 1 mm pitch". Electrical rule 1 demands a
≥ 0.3 mm web between antipads. FAB.md gives 0.6 mm pads and 0.2 mm hole-to-copper. With the unused L2 pad kept, the
antipad is 0.6 + 2 x 0.2 = **1.0 mm at a 1.0 mm pitch, so the web is 0**. That is a continuous slot in L2 directly
under the commutation loops that rule 4 protects.

The same arithmetic applies to U1's outward fan-out row (critique §8: 0.4/0.2 mm signal vias). At a 0.5-0.65 mm
pitch the antipads (0.6-0.8 mm) merge into a moat around the MCU in L2, and in L3, which is L4's reference.

**Correction.**

- Enable "remove unused inner pads" for non-GND vias on L2/L3. The antipad becomes the drill + 0.4 mm, which leaves a
  0.3 mm web at a 1.0 mm pitch for 0.3 mm drills.
- Or use a ≥ 1.3 mm pitch.
- Stagger fan-out vias in two rows with a same-row pitch of ≥ 0.9 mm.
- Keep VBAT via fields on the outboard side of the drain tabs, not in the cap-to-shunt loop footprint.
- Add a **plane-integrity check** to the routability gate: the minimum L2 and L3-GND web, and no antipad chain
  longer than 2 vias.

### 3. The R302/R402 branches have no legal layer (major)

**Evidence.** Rule 8 feeds U3/U4 "from C1 on its own branch, ≥ 1.0 mm on L1/L3". The floorplan puts C1 at the
front-left and U4 at the rear-right. The rules say L3 is GND everywhere outside the front band, and L1 in the rear is
full of logic. A 50-60 mm, 1 mm VBAT trace on L3 across the rear slots the L4 reference exactly where the MCU
fan-out runs. That is the same failure class as v1's L3 east bus.

**Correction.** Plan the branch at floorplan time as a named corridor. Two options:

- On L3 along the right board edge, with L4 GND fill under it and no L4 crossings.
- On L1 where R402 sits beside the band.

Or reinterpret the "own branch" rule: the intent is to keep bridge di/dt out of VM. A tap off the L3 feed at its
C1-side end, before any cell, does that. Make the geometry explicit in DESIGN §6.6.

### 4. L3 lanes have no usable reference unless L4 above them is GND (major)

**Evidence.** L2 is 1.23 mm from L3 (§0). Electrical rule D allows "few slow lanes, only where no L4 signal runs
above". An L3 trace with L4 empty or routed above it references L2 through 1.2 mm. That means high loop
inductance, and broadside coupling of 0.07 mm to anything on L4.

**Correction.** An L3 lane is legal only with **solid L4 GND fill directly over it** (≥ 3 x its width), stitched at
both ends. Count those L4 fill strips as lost L4 capacity in the routability gate.

### 5. Pack path before RS4 is L1-only (major)

**Evidence.** Rule 2 sizes L1 + L3 in parallel (≥ 5 mm per layer, ~1.5 mΩ, 33 mV at 22 A). Only VBAT has an L3
pour. BAT_IN → Q7 → PSW_S → Q8 → VBAT_SW → RS4 sits in the front-left on L1 alone, and it is also where Q7+Q8 put
2.0-2.5 W into the same copper (calcs §11). RS4's land alone limits the width to 4 mm (DESIGN §6.12).

**Correction.**

- Duplicate BAT_IN, PSW_S and VBAT_SW as L3 islands under Q7/Q8, with ≥ 8 vias per tab. This spreads the heat too.
  L3 there is outside the L4 signal zone if the pack entry stays a self-contained block.
- Size the rest by IPC-2152 rather than squares. At 1 oz (35 µm) external, 5 mm carries ~10 A rms at ~10-15 °C
  rise before neighbouring heat. Fine for the average, but not with 2 W of FET heat underneath unless L3 shares it.
- Wire-hole spokes: JBAT1/JBAT2 spokes on L1 and L3 (BAT_IN) and on L2/L3/L4 (GND). 4 x 1 mm per layer is fine
  for 22 A bursts.

### 6. Thermal (major)

**Evidence.**

- Rule 15 asks for "an exposed bottom GND pad" under U3/U4. The same rule and E3-09 say the drive ICs' thermal copper
  must not face the Pico, and L4 faces the compute board 4.9-6 mm away.
- RθJA 25.7 °C/W assumes a 76 x 114 mm 2s2p board with two full planes. Here the heat path is EP → vias → **one
  30 µm plane (L2)** plus a 10 x 10 mm L3 island.
- A 30 µm sheet spreads at 1/(k·t) ≈ 85 K/W per square. Realistic RθJA is 35-45 °C/W. At the 1.5 A worst case
  (1.96 W) that is +70-90 K over a board already +50 K after a match (E3-09), which reaches OTW at 135 °C.

**Correction.**

- Resolve the contradiction: either no exposed bottom pad (tented bottom, heat on L1/L2/L3), or an exposed pad with
  a matching keep-out on the compute board. Record which.
- Add L1 GND copper around U3/U4 (the PGND pins and a top-side flood). With hot parts on top, this is the main
  spreader.
- Add a 4-layer thermal estimate (a spreading calc or a quick FEM) to calcs.py before placement freezes via counts.
- 1.2 mm helps a little: vias are 25 % shorter.

### 7. 1.2 mm versus 1.6 mm (major)

**Evidence.** The README offers 1.2 mm "to save ~2-3 g" (0.4 mm x 29.75 cm² x 1.85 g/cm³ ≈ 2.2 g). Bending
stiffness scales with t³: (1.2/1.6)³ = 0.42, so **2.4x the deflection** for the same impact. The board carries
C1 (10 x 10.5 mm), 2512s, 1206 MLCCs across an unfused pack (DESIGN §6.10 flex-crack rule), 16-18 AWG pack leads
and a 1.27 mm board-to-board header.

**Correction.**

- Default to **1.6 mm (JLC041611-1080)**.
- If the weight budget forces 1.2 mm, use JLC041211-1080 or -3313, never -2116 (0.193 mm prepreg). Specify
  soft-termination MLCCs for every VBAT-connected 1206/0805, and add a standoff near C1.

### 8. The inner planes do not shield low-frequency magnetic fields (major)

**Evidence.** The skin depth in copper is ~65 µm at 1 MHz and ~21 µm at 10 MHz. L2/L3 at 30 µm are magnetically
transparent to the 24 kHz phase current, its PWM ripple and the chopped bus current below ~10 MHz. The weapon phase
current returns through the motor, not through L2, so it gets no image cancellation. Rule 11's "≥ 1 mm from phase,
SW and gate nets" reads as same-layer.

**Correction.** Keep SPx/SNx, SOx, NTC and phase-divider loops out from under the phase strips, the wire holes and
the C1-to-cell feed on **every** layer. Minimise pair loop area. Route the Kelvin pairs from the shunts toward U2,
perpendicular to the phase-current path.

### 9. Copper-resistance numbers (minor)

The memo uses 0.49 mΩ/sq for inner 1 oz. JLC's finished inner copper is 30 µm, which is **0.57 mΩ/sq**
(0.69 hot). Its 0.5 oz is 15.2 µm, which is 1.13 mΩ/sq. Rule 2's 1.5 mΩ becomes ~1.6 mΩ at 25 °C and ~1.9 mΩ hot:
~42 mV at 22 A. That is still fine, but fix the numbers.

### 10. The "no 2 oz" argument (minor)

JLC's multilayer 2 oz outer minimum is **0.15/0.15 mm**. The 0.5 mm-pitch QFN/LQFP pads have ~0.2 mm gaps, so
2 oz does not rule out the fine-pitch parts. What 2 oz does cost:

- the 0.127 mm logic routing (FAB.md);
- etch compensation on the pads;
- ~1.1-1.5 g (both outer layers, ~60 % fill).

2 oz **inner** (JLC041612-3313) forces a 0.2 mm prepreg, which defeats the thin L1-L2. The conclusion stands; fix
the reason. Two oz is not needed for resistance (finding 9). Its only real case would be thermal spreading on L1
(finding 6).

### 11. Via-in-pad (minor)

JLC POFV is free only on 6-20 layers and charged on 4L. U2/U3/U4's EPs, RS1-RS3's 6-8 in-pad vias and the
bottom-tenting conflict (finding 6) all argue for simply **ordering POFV** and recording the cost in FAB.md.
Tented 0.2 mm vias in a 0.5 mm-pitch VQFN EP wick solder and void the joint.

### 12. The two documents disagree on L3 in the rear (minor)

Critique §8 puts signal channels and +3V3/+5V on L3 in the rear. README §3 and electrical rule D say L3 GND there.
The MCU fan-out lives in the rear, so the critique version removes L4's reference exactly where it is needed. Mark
critique §8's L3 sentence as superseded, and state the rail layers (+5V to J1 and +3V3 trunks) as **L1/L4** only.

### 13. Stitch vias per signal via (minor)

"A GND stitch via within ~1 mm of every signal via" makes ~130 extra vias against a budget of ~1.1 per connection.
Require 1:1 only for fast or aggressive edges (PWM, SPI_SCK, gate, INH). Otherwise allow one stitch per group of
≤ 3-4 adjacent signal vias within 1.5 mm. Count them in the via budget.

### 14. C1 ripple share (minor)

Finding 1(a) lowers the C1-to-cell inductance about tenfold, so C1 takes a larger share of the ~8 A rms ripple.
Its rating is 2.8 A rms (DESIGN §3.1). Re-run sim_weapon_bridge or spinup with the v2 feed inductance and check
C1's share before committing.

## Missing requirements (add to README §3)

- A named stack-up and a KiCad STACKUP4 matching **JLC041611-1080**: 0.069 / 1.23 / 0.069, Cu 35/30/30/35.
- Plane-integrity DRC: the minimum web in L2 and L3-GND, and no L4 trace across an L3 net boundary.
- A front-band L4 policy (finding 1), and corridors for the drive VM branches and the rails (findings 3 and 12).
- A 4-layer thermal estimate and a bottom-pad decision (finding 6).
- Re-run the commutation-loop estimate at 0.069 mm and the plane-drop solve including L1/L3/L4 GND copper.
- Order POFV on the 4-layer board; confirm the stack-up name on the order form.
