| N-01 | NOTE | **Current polarity and register settings (asked for in scope).**  The DRV8323 CSA **inverts**: I = (V_VREF/2 − V_SOx)/(G·R), where I flows from SPx (FET source) to SNx (GND).  All three shunts behave the same way.  **Shoot-through** (VBAT → HS → LS → shunt → GND), a **phase-to-phase short** (current enters the low-side-on phase and goes down its shunt), a **phase-to-VBAT short** with the LS on, and motor current *returning* through a low side all push SP above SN, so SOx falls toward 0.25 V.  The trip is therefore SOx < VREF/2 − 1.2 V = **0.45 V** (30 A).  With INP = SOx and INM = DAC, COMP's natural (POL = 0) output goes **low** on a fault.  TIM1 needs BKP = 0 for the active-low nFAULT/ALERT on PC13, and BKP is shared by the ORed break sources, so the comparator inputs are active-low with BKCMP1P = BKCMP3P = 0.  COMP6 has no BKCMP6P and needs no inversion.  So: **POL = 0 on all three, BKCMPxP = 0, no inversion anywhere**.  A comparator that is disabled outputs 0 and holds the break, which is fail-safe.  Current *sourced* from GND through a low side (the other polarity) is caught only through the other two phases: 30–60 A peak depending on angle (same as R10D-02).  INMSEL: COMP1 and COMP3 both get **DAC3_CH1** (100) or DAC1_CH1 (101).  COMP6 gets **DAC4_CH2** (100) or DAC2_CH1 (101).  DAC1_OUT1 is PA4 (W_VA) and DAC2_OUT1 is PA6 (W_NTC), so use DAC3_CH1 + DAC4_CH2 (internal-only), or run DAC1/DAC2 in an internal-only MODE.  Sharing DAC3_CH1 between COMP1 and COMP3 is fine (same threshold) | [DRV23] Eq. 3, Fig. 8-26/8-27; [RM] Table 196/197 (COMP1 INP0 = PA1, COMP3 INP0 = PA0, COMP6 INP0 = PB11), TIMx_AF1 bits 1–7 BKCMP1E–BKCMP7E and 10–13 BKCMP1P–BKCMP4P ("must be programmed together with the BKP polarity bit"); [DB] PA0 COMP3_INP, PA1 COMP1_INP, PB11 COMP6_INP. | N-02 | NOTE (firmware, but the trip depends on it) | **Break mechanics in the FOC configuration.**  §8 holds CHxN high through CCxNP = 1 (the inactive level), with CCxNE = 0 and OSSR = 1. INL only falls "if the clock is present … OCxN = OISxN after a dead-time" (+ ~2 clocks of MOE resync), and **that step is valid only for tim_brk**.  Three consequences: (a) route the comparators to **BRK (BKCMPxE), never BRK2 (BK2CMPxE)**.  On BRK2 the CHxN pins stay at CCxNP = high, so the weapon phases stay **enabled** in FOC mode.
