| R21A-01 | **MAJOR** | [D] l.673 "drives resume" (brake window), drive rows 1/2 (l.677–678), §9 step 6 (l.761) | **The resume brake window will trip the DRV8316 OCP whenever a drive resumes with its wheel turning faster than about 1–2 m/s.  The fault policy then turns that trip into a supply-budget hold, or a drive latch.  The text treats it as a bench check that should pass.  By the numbers it is expected to fail, and nothing says what firmware does when it does.**<br><br>**(1) The window is a 3-phase short.**  Between lowering the DRV_OFF pin and setting MOE, TIM8/TIM20 idle at OISx = 1, so INH is high on all three phases.  INL is tied to +3V3, so every high side is on.  In 3x mode there is no Hi-Z except through DRVOFF or the CTRL4 bit.  The minimum window is ~1 ms (the nFAULT check).<br><br>**(2) The current exceeds the OCP threshold.**  Mk4.1 data [C §10]: Kt = 2.73 mN·m/A, 6 pole pairs, 46.5 k rpm at the duty cap, R_LL ~0.2–0.3 Ω (estimate).  That gives λ_phase ≈ 0.26 mWb.  For a short, I ≈ ωλ/√(R² + (ωL)²).  I assumed L_phase 5–20 µH, plus ~0.1 Ω for the HS FET.  Steady-state phase current:<br>• 46.5 k rpm (3.7 m/s): **12–31 A**<br>• 38.7 k rpm: 12–27 A<br>• 25 k rpm (~2 m/s): 10–19 A<br>• 15 k rpm (~1.2 m/s): 8–12 A<br>OCP_LVL 0 trips at **10 / 16 / 22 A** min/typ/max [DRV Table 7.5, IOCP].  The current rises at E/L ≈ 0.4–1.5 A/µs, and τ = L/R ≈ 25–100 µs.  So it reaches steady state (and a transient overshoot on top) well inside a 1 ms window.  The 0.6 µs OCP deglitch filters nothing here.<br><br>**(3) The policy turns the trip into a hold or a latch.**  CTRL4 0x0C90/0x0D10 sets OCP_MODE = 00, latched [DRV Table 8-21, Table 8-8].  An OCP during resume is "expected, not an event".  Then:<br>• After ~1 ms, nFAULT is still low, so the fault recovery runs CLR_FLT.<br>• MOE is still 0, so the brake re-applies and re-trips within tens of µs.<br>• nFAULT never stays high for the ≤ 5 ms wait, so the chip is "classified through this table".<br>• **Both drives at speed** (the usual case: link blip, bounce/supply restart, MCU reset mid-match, switch-open clear): drive row 1 "both DRV8316s at once" → **Supply**.  That repeats every ~25 ms, so the supply budget's > 5/s rule holds **everything, weapon included, off for ~1 s**.  Four such episodes in a minute reach > 20 → "supply unstable" hold until an operator clears it.<br>• **One drive at speed** (turning): row 2 OCP → coast, rewrite, Release → resume, which brakes again.  More than 3 in a second **latches that drive** (persisted across resets).<br>After a supply event, [D] l.673 also resumes the drives — at speed if the robot is moving.
