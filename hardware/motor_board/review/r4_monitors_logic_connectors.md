Scope: U8 BQ76907 + balance input, U7 INA229, U6 74LVC1G08 interlock, J1 B2B header, J2/J3/J4,
D3 power LED, every footprint string in `design/motor_board.py`.  Reviewer stance: adversarial, primary
sources only.  No design files were edited.

| R4-01 | **MAJOR** | U8 VC0 / CELL0, GND, Q7 | **VC0 goes below VSS by more than the −0.3 V abs max for long periods (DC), whenever the balance lead is plugged in and the main switch is open.** That is the normal parked state, because DESIGN §3.1 puts the switch in the + wire and says U8 "runs whenever the balance lead is plugged in". U8's ~150 µA supply current enters BAT from BAL4 and leaves VSS into board GND. With the switch open, GND can only get back to the pack negative through (a) Q7's body diode (anode = GND, cathode = BAT_NEG; the channel is off because VBAT floats) or (b) U8's own VSS→VC0 ESD diode plus R6 to BAL0. Either way GND sits about 0.35–0.5 V above BAL0, so VC0 ≈ VSS − 0.4 V. The same happens (i) at every balance-lead hot plug with the main unplugged: C9 and the board's GND node charge through the VC0 ESD diode, peak about 16.8 V/(R6+R11) ≈ 150 mA for about 100 µs; (ii) with a reversed main pack and the balance lead plugged: GND ≈ pack− + one D1 forward drop. TI's random-connection guarantee does not cover this board: here VC0 reaches VSS only through C8, never through a resistor to VSS on the PCB. While the main is live, GND − BAL0 = I × (neg lead + XT30 + Q7 + copper), estimated at about 5 mΩ, so about −0.11 V at 22 A and −0.2 V (the recommended-operation minimum) at about 40 A. | DS 6.1: "VC0 … VSS–0.3 … VSS+6 V". DS 6.3: VVC(0) min −0.2 V. DS 8.2.4 Note: "VC0 must be connected through the series-cell input resistor to the VSS pin on the PCB, before any cells are attached … If VC0 is not connected through the series resistor to VSS on the PCB, then cells cannot be connected in random sequence." DS Table 8-3: "VC0 must be connected through a resistor and capacitor on the PCB to pin 11 (VSS)". Netlist: CELL0 = C4.2, C8.1, R6.2, U8.5 only (`design/nets.md`). | Add **D5: Schottky, anode = GND, cathode = CELL0** (after R6, next to U8 pin 5). | R4-02 | **MAJOR** | U6 → U2 ENABLE (W_ENABLE), DESIGN §8 "Boot order" | **The DRV8323 cannot be configured before W_ARM is asserted, and it loses its configuration every time W_ARM drops.** Low ENABLE (U6 output low whenever W_ARM or W_EN is low) puts U2 into sleep: SPI is disabled and all registers reset to defaults. Each ENABLE transition also holds nFAULT low, which trips TIM1_BKIN (and on the same net, the INA229 alert path). | DRV8323 §8.4.1.1: "In sleep mode, all gate drivers are disabled … and the SPI bus is disabled." DRV8323 line 4335: "The SPI registers are reset to the default settings on power up, when the device enters sleep mode". DRV8323 line 4256: "During power up and power down of the device through the ENABLE pin, the nFAULT pin is held low". Netlist: W_ENABLE = U6.4 → U2.33; W_ARM = J1.7, R41, U6.2 only. | Hardware can stay as is (the interlock is right), but rewrite the firmware contract in DESIGN §8. (5) Only then clear the break and start PWM. On any TIM1 break or SPI read-back mismatch, go back to step 1. ## VERIFIED

**U8 BQ76907 (DS Table 5-1, Table 7-1, Table 8-3)**
- Pin numbers and names match DS Table 5-1 exactly: 1 VC4, 2 VC3, 3 VC2, 4 VC1, 5 VC0, 6 SRP, 7 SRN, 8 TS, 9 DSG, 10 CHG, 11 VSS, 12 SCL, 13 SDA, 14 ALERT, 15 REGOUT, 16 REGSRC, 17 BAT, 18 VC7, 19 VC6, 20 VC5. EP = pad 21 (RGR0020A "EXPOSED PAD 21"; EVM ties EP to VSS) → GND.
- 4S wiring matches DS Table 7-1 / TRM Table 5-8 (Vcell Mode 4). CELL4 → VC7; CELL3 → VC6 = VC5; CELL2 → VC4 = VC3; CELL1 → VC2 = VC1; CELL0 → VC0. Cells are VC7–VC6, VC5–VC4, VC3–VC2 and VC1–VC0; shorts are VC6 = VC5, VC4 = VC3 and VC2 = VC1. The top and bottom cells use VC7–VC6 and VC1–VC0 as Table 8-3 requires. VC5/VC6 ≥ 2 V holds (VC5 = 3 cells ≈ 9–12.6 V).
- Shorted pins share one resistor and are joined at the IC side. This matches DS Fig 7-4 and EVM §4.5: "short the VCx pins at the capacitor and remove the unused input resistor".
- Filter topology: differential caps across each used cell plus VC0 → VSS match DS Fig 8-1 (differential chain + VC0 cap to VSS). TI additionally shows VC1 → VSS instead of a VC1–VC0 differential cap; both are acceptable. R = 100 Ω is within 10–1000 Ω; RC = 10 µs ≤ 200 µs (capacitor value: see R4-06).
- Unused-pin termination matches Table 8-3: SRP/SRN/TS → VSS (GND); DSG/CHG open; ALERT used; REGSRC = BAT (see R4-05 for REGOUT).
- I²C levels: VIH min 1.23 V, VIL max 0.53 V and inputs up to 5.5 V (DS 6.6, 6.3) are fixed thresholds, not referenced to REGOUT. 3.3 V pull-ups on the compute board are therefore fine with REGOUT unused. Pins tolerate VSS + 6 V when U8 is unpowered (DS 6.1: ALERT/SCL/SDA VSS − 0.3 … VSS + 6).
- The BAT supply current flowing through the BAL4 wire causes a negligible VC7 error (µV).

**U7 INA229 (INA229 Table 5-1)**: 1 CS, 2 MOSI, 3 ALERT, 4 MISO, 5 SCLK, 6 VS, 7 GND, 8 VBUS, 9 IN−, 10 IN+ match the netlist exactly. High-side orientation is correct: IN+ → VBAT_PACK (supply side of RS4) and IN− → VBAT (load side). MISO tri-states when CS is high (tCS_MISO_HIZ 25 ns), so sharing SPI3 MISO is OK. ALERT is not asserted at reset (R4-13).

**U6 74LVC1G08SE-7 (Diodes DS32199, SOT353)**: pinout 1 A, 2 B, 3 GND, 4 Y, 5 VCC matches the netlist and KiCad `SOT-353_SC-70-5` (pads 1–3 left top-to-bottom, 4–5 right bottom-to-top). Inputs are 5.5 V tolerant and IOFF-rated. When +3V3 is absent the output goes Hi-Z (IOFF) and the DRV8323 ENABLE internal pull-down (DRV8323 6.5: "RPD Pulldown resistance To AGND 100 kΩ") holds ENABLE low, so the gate driver stays asleep. R40/R41 100 kΩ pull-downs: 74LVC input leakage (µA) × 100 kΩ ≪ VIL 0.8 V. Y drives ENABLE with VOH ≈ 3.3 V > DRV8323 VIH 1.5 V.

The vendor drawing confirms 1.27 pitch, gull-wing SMD, "额定电流 3A" and contact resistance ≤ 20 mΩ. The KiCad footprint exists; its pads are compatible (R4-10). - PQFN-8-EP_6x5 Generic: pads 1–4 plus the tab as "5". The netlist uses 1–3 S, 4 G, 5 D. The FET pin mapping itself is out of scope here and was not re-verified.

## Summary for the design owner

BLOCKER: none.
MAJOR:
- **R4-01**: VC0 is driven below the abs-max VSS − 0.3 V in the parked state (switch open, balance lead plugged), on balance-lead hot plug, and with a reversed main. Fix: a Schottky from GND to CELL0.
- **R4-02**: the DRV8323 sleeps and resets its SPI configuration to IDRIVE max / 6x mode whenever W_ARM or W_EN is low. The §8 boot order is impossible and must be replaced by a configure-after-wake procedure with read-back.
- **R4-03**: three footprint names do not exist. The DRV8316 RGF 5×7 needs a custom footprint (EP ≈ 3.7 × 5.7 per TI).
