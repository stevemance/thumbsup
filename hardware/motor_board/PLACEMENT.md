# Motor board placement (v2, two-sided)

Board **75 x 35 mm (2625 mm2)**, 4 copper layers (DESIGN 6.1/6.2 stack-up unchanged), parts on both
sides. Placement only: no copper is drawn. Generated by `kicad/gen/place_v2.py` from `kicad/gen/placement_spec.py`
(the spec holds every decision and its reason). Checked by `kicad/gen/critical.py` (pin distances) and
`kicad/gen/measure.py` (gaps, edges, loops, flex orientation); KiCad DRC: no courtyard overlaps, no errors,
schematic parity 0.

Coordinates below: board top view, origin at the **front-left** corner, x toward the robot's right, y toward the
**rear**. The front edge faces the drum.

## 1. Size and how it was reached

- Parts' courtyards total ~2,770 mm2; through-hole parts (wire holes, mounting holes, J4) block both sides.
  At 35 x 75 the top side is 64 % covered by courtyards and the bottom 45 %.
- 35 mm is the short side. The long side went 75 -> 71.5 -> 75: at 71.5 the top still fitted (67 %), but the
  rear half of the bottom could not hold the MCU with its ring of decoupling and CSA filters, J1, J2, J3 and the
  drive-R bulk caps. The front half of the bottom is not usable for those, because the pack return current
  flows there (DESIGN 6.2). At 75 mm everything fits with its critical parts at the pins.
- Squarer is not possible under the 35 mm limit without taking parts off the board (area / 35 = ~72-75 mm).

## 2. How it sits in the robot

- **Front edge (drum):** the three weapon wire holes JW1-JW3, in front of their phase cells. The weapon leads
  go straight forward.
- **Rear edge (drive motors directly behind):** drive-left wire holes JL1-JL3 at the rear-left corner, the left
  sensor connector J2 (bottom) next to them; drive-right wire holes JR1-JR3 on the right edge near the rear-right
  corner, the right sensor connector J3 (bottom) on the rear edge beside them. Each drive motor's leads and
  sensor cable leave from the corner that faces it.
- **Left end (battery):** BAT+ / BAT- holes (JBAT1, JBAT2, 6 mm apart so the pair stays twisted) and the
  balance connector J4 (mating face flush with the left edge). The pack sits to the left of the board, or its
  leads are brought round to that end. If the battery ends up on the right, the whole board mirrors: nothing in
  the floorplan depends on it being the left.
- **Power LED D3:** top side, rear edge (visible from behind/above; move it if the chassis hides that edge).

### Mounting (3D-printed PLA chassis)
Four M2 NPTH holes, MH1-MH4, at (3.05, 3.05), (72.25, 3.05), (3.05, 32.05), (72.05, 32.05), a 69 x 29 mm
rectangle. They carry the whole stack (DESIGN 3.5: nylon standoffs between the two boards, the header carries no
load):

- Print four bosses on the chassis floor at the hole pattern with **M2 heat-set brass inserts** (PLA takes
  them well; a clearance hole in a solid 6 mm boss if you prefer a nut trap).
- Stack from the chassis up: boss -> **soft washer** (1 mm silicone/EPDM, or a rubber grommet) -> compute board ->
  nylon standoff (length = the mated J1 height, see 5) -> this board -> nylon washer -> M2 nylon or steel screw into
  the insert. The soft washers keep impact shock out of the boards (DESIGN 3.5: mount the stack soft, not hanging
  on the M2 nylon alone); a strip of foam under the compute board adds damping.
- Nothing is mechanically attached to the wire holes or connectors; add strain relief (a printed clip or tie
  point) on the chassis for the battery and weapon leads within ~20 mm of the board (DESIGN 6.9).
- C1 (10.5 mm tall) is staked to the board with adhesive after assembly (DESIGN 6.10); leave ~2 mm above it.

## 3. Floorplan, top side (hot, tall and high-current parts)

- **Pack path, left to right along the front:** JBAT1 -> Q7 (drain tab against JBAT1) -> Q7/Q8 sources face to
  face (common source PSW_S, U13 above them) -> Q8 drain tab -> RS4 -> VBAT. C1 sits behind RS4 with its VBAT pad
  toward the bridge; D1 (TVS) in the front strip in front of RS4's VBAT end. From C1 the VBAT pour runs right on
  L1/L3 to the bridge; the return comes back on the L2 plane to JBAT2. Everything this current touches is in the
  front-left and front strip; the MCU, the CSA filters and J1 are all behind it (DESIGN 6.2).
- **Weapon bridge (front right):** three identical "U-cells", left to right phase C, B, A (the order U2's pins
  come out in). In each cell the low-side FET (drain tab forward) and high-side FET (source pins forward) stand
  side by side, so the phase node is one short strip across their front edges with the phase wire hole right in
  front of it. The low side's source pins face its shunt directly behind it; the local 10 uF stands behind the
  high side's drain tab with its GND pad 0.75 mm from the shunt's GND pad. The commutation loop
  (cap -> HS -> phase -> LS -> shunt -> cap) is ~32 mm long through the pad centres and encloses ~53 mm2, on L1 with
  the L2 plane directly under it (DESIGN 6.1). Kelvin ties NT1-NT3 sit at the shunts' GND pads, TH1 in the gap
  behind phase B's high-side drain.
- **Weapon driver U2 (behind the bridge):** rotated 180 so the phase B/C gate and sense pins face the cells in
  front and the phase A pins face cell A on the right. The straps and DVDD/VREF are on its left, the buck pins
  at its rear-right corner with the buck as one block behind it (L1 SW pad under SW pin 45, D2 beside it, C29/C30
  bridging D2's anode and L1's +5V pad).
- **Drive left (rear left):** R302 left of C1 (fed from C1 on its own branch), U3 behind C1 with its VM pins
  facing forward to R302 and its phase outputs facing the JL holes. 100 nF VM caps, CP and AVDD caps at the pins
  on top.
- **Drive right (rear right):** U4 rotated 90 so its outputs face the JR holes on the right edge; its VM and CP
  caps in the row behind it; R402 on the rear edge behind that row, fed from C1 on its own L3 branch (not
  through the bridge copper, DESIGN 6.6).
- **Middle rear:** the LDO U5 and its caps, the test pads (all on top, reachable with the compute board mounted,
  DESIGN 6.9), D3.

## 4. Floorplan, bottom side (cool, low parts)

- MCU U1 centre-rear, its decoupling and the SOx / divider / NTC filters around its pins. J1 to its right.
- Cell monitor U8 and its input network under J4; INA239 U7 under RS4 (Kelvin through vias from the pad inner
  edges); the power-switch gate network (R1, D10, C13, R32, D4, R14) under Q7/Q8/U13.
- Drive bulk caps (4 x 10 uF each) under the VM pins of U3 and U4; the drive ICs' unused-buck resistor/cap under
  their SW_BK/FB_BK pins.
- Sensor channels next to J2 and J3 at the rear edge; the supply switches U11/U12 kept ~10 mm from the drive ICs
  (85 C parts, DESIGN 6.5).
- Interlock U6 and the ARM circuit under the bridge's rear strip (VBAT/GND copper above, no switching node),
  between U2's INL pins, the MCU and J1's W_ARM_CLK end, away from MB_TX.
- Phase-divider top resistors under their wire holes (only the low-impedance divider node runs to the MCU).

## 5. Sides, heights and the stack

**Rule used:** anything that dissipates, carries pack current, touches BAT_IN, or is taller than the board gap is on
top; loop-critical parts stay on their IC's side; everything else is on the bottom. Measured heights (datasheets):
top tallest C1 10.5 mm, J4 6.1, L1 4.0, D1/D2 2.5; **bottom tallest J2/J3 3.35 mm**, then 1206 caps 1.8, the MCU
1.6; everything else on the bottom is <= 1.3 mm.

**Board gap:** set by the mated height of J1 (BOOMELE male 5.5 mm nominal + the socket you choose; DESIGN 3.5
estimates 4.9-6.0 mm). The compute board must keep clear:
- under J2 and J3 (3.35 mm, rear edge), plus their cable plugs' approach from the rear;
- under every wire-hole solder joint (they protrude on the bottom): JBAT1/2, JW1-3, JL1-3, JR1-3, and J4's five
  pins (DESIGN 6.9) - clear zones or cut-outs on the compute board;
- elsewhere its top-side parts must be shorter than the gap minus 1.8 mm.

**Deviations from DESIGN 6.13's side list** (that list assumed a ~1.1 mm bottom height limit; the user relaxed it
to "choose the standoff, hot parts on top"):
- U1 (MCU, ~0.26 W, 1.6 mm) and its CSA filters: bottom. 6.13 listed U1 on top.
- C9 (1206) and D5 (SOD-123) with U8: bottom (they were top only for height).
- J2/J3: bottom, at the rear edge (3.35 mm; compute board keep-out).
- The drive ICs' 4 x 10 uF VM bulk caps: bottom, directly under the VM pins through vias; the 100 nF HF caps stay
  on top at the pins. 6.13 says driver decoupling stays on its IC's side; the bulk caps are the RC filter with
  R302/R402, not the HF loop. **Review item.**
- R300/C307 and R400/C407 (unused-buck resistor mode, <= ~0.05 W until firmware sets BUCK_DIS): bottom.
- The power-switch gate network (not BAT_IN): bottom. U13, C12, C14, R13 (BAT_IN) stay on top.

**Flex-crack rule (DESIGN 6.10):** every 0805/1206/2512 part placed by rule runs parallel to its nearest edge and
is >= 3 mm from the mounting holes (`measure.py orient`). Exceptions, both in the bridge: the 10 uF caps C26/C31
stand across the board (they only fit vertically between the shunts), and RS1 runs along the board while its
nearest edge is the right one. On a 75 x 35 board held at its corners the dominant bending strain runs along the
75 mm length, which a cap standing across the board sees least; they are 16-28 mm from the holes. **Review item.**

## 6. JLCPCB assembly

- Standard PCBA, double-sided, 4 layers: supported. The board is under 70 x 70 mm, so JLC adds edge rails; ask
  for rails on all four edges (the 2.5 mm body-to-edge rule is then measured to the rails; J2-J4 and the wire
  holes sit at the board edges).
- Smallest pad-to-pad gap between different parts is well above JLC's minimum spacing table (0402 0.18 /
  0603 0.25 / 1206 0.35 / SOT 0.4 / QFN-QFN 1.0 mm): see `measure.py gaps`.
- The wire holes, test pads, net ties, solder jumpers and mounting holes are out of the BOM (hand-soldered or
  copper only).
- Fiducials: JLC adds them.

## 7. Not decided by placement

Copper: the L1/L3 VBAT pours, via fields at the HS tabs and caps, the gate-via escapes (each high-side gate pin
sits at the front-left of its FET, beside the phase strip), the AGND islands, net classes and the real design
rules (the project currently has placeholder JLC values).
